# Deterministic Explore Trial Artifacts

These files preserve generated effects and scorecards from the removed Aider/Ollama Explore experiment. They are historical model outputs; factual claims in them were not independently certified by archiving.

## Results

- `STATIC-LEDGER-SCORECARD.md`: deterministic static evidence trial, 89/100.
- `PORTED-SCORECARD.md`: ported Aider Explore trial, 50/100.
- `LUNA-LOW-SCORECARD.md`: Luna-low comparison, 79/100.
- `PORTED-SUMMARY.md` and `PORTED-03-micro.md`: generated Planner-facing output from the 50/100 trial, retained as an example of a structurally acceptable but factually unreliable report.

The strategy and caveats are summarized in [`../../DETERMINISTIC_EXPLORE_STRATEGY.md`](../../DETERMINISTIC_EXPLORE_STRATEGY.md).
